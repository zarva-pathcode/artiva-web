Elena
Vostrova

verified Verified Artist
workspace_premium Digital COA

Capturing the ephemeral dance between light and stone, Elena's work explores the boundaries of form and negative space. Her signature 'Silent Echo' series has been featured in galleries across Europe, challenging the viewer to find weight in the invisible.

Born in the historic streets of Siena and now creating from her sun-drenched studio in Milan, Vostrova merges classical Italian sculpting techniques with a brutalist modern sensibility. She works primarily with Carrara marble and reclaimed industrial steel.
